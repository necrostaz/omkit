ALREADY INSTALLED OMKit BEFORE (and no official firmware since)?
  Do NOT flash the camera again. Skip part A and go straight to part B.


A. FLASH THE CAMERA (only once)
-------------------------------
 1. IMPORTANT: the camera must run EXACTLY firmware 1.1 (the camera's
    menu shows the version; see its manual).
    NEVER flash these files over any other firmware version: this can
    brick the camera.
      older than 1.1: first update to 1.1 the official way (OM Workspace).
      newer than 1.1: STOP. This release is not for your camera.
    Charge the battery fully.
 2. Extract (unzip) this whole ZIP into a folder on the computer.
    Do not run anything from inside the ZIP.
 3. You need Python 3.9 or newer on the computer (python.org).
 4. Start the firmware tool in the folder "firmware_tool":
      Windows:  double-click  get_firmware_windows.bat
      macOS:    double-click  get_firmware_macos.command
                (if macOS refuses to open it: open Terminal, type
                "bash " with a space, drag the file into the window
                and press Enter)
      Linux:    in a terminal:  sh get_firmware_linux.sh
    Press Enter when asked. It downloads the official firmware, checks it,
    patches it and checks the result. Wait for "Done."
    It writes two folders into firmware_tool/output:
      patched/   - to install OMKit (next step)
      original/  - the official firmware, to go back (part C). KEEP IT.
 5. Take a memory card WITHOUT an "OMKIT" folder (format it in the camera).
    Copy the folder  output/patched/DCOLYMP  to the root of the card.
    The card now holds:  DCOLYMP\E1489999.BIN
 6. Switch the camera OFF and put the card in.
    HOLD the OK button and switch the camera ON while holding OK.
 7. Wait and watch the front lamp (the screen stays dark):
      lamp lit STEADILY = flashing in progress. Do NOT touch the camera:
                          never switch off, open a cover or remove the
                          battery or card.
      lamp BLINKING     = flashing finished. Now you may switch off.
 8. Switch the camera off, then on again (do NOT hold OK).
    Check that it starts normally, as before.
    If it does not start normally: go to part C, "Back to the stock
    firmware".
 9. Switch the camera off. On the computer DELETE the folder DCOLYMP from
    the card (or format the card in the camera).
10. Go on with part B.


B. COPY OR UPDATE OMKit ON THE CARD
-----------------------------------
 First time:
 1. Copy the folder "OMKIT" of this release to the root of the card.
 2. Put the card in the camera and switch it on. Done: the modules are
    already on.
    In the picture mode screen you now find "Film Look" in place of
    i-Enhance, and Color/Mono Profiles 1-3.

 Updating (OMKit is on the card already):
 1. Read this release's section in CHANGELOG.md first: it says if the
    update needs more than copying files.
 2. Copy the FILES inside this release's OMKIT folders onto the card's
    OMKIT folders, replacing old ones:
      OMKIT/CORE.BIN, OMKIT/MODULES/*, OMKIT/FILMLOOK/*, OMKIT/GRAIN/*
    Do NOT copy OMKIT.INI - your own file keeps your settings.
    (On macOS copy the files, not the folders: replacing a folder would
    delete your own presets.)
 3. Put the card in the camera and switch it on.

 Your own presets (.ISP) in OMKIT/FILMLOOK stay; delete the bundled ones
 you do not want.


C. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG / GOING BACK
---------------------------------------
 - Start without OMKit: take the card out, or hold OK while switching on
   and keep holding it for a few seconds (only with NO DCOLYMP folder on
   the card, or OK starts a firmware update).
 - Back to the stock firmware: do steps A5-A9 with  output/original/DCOLYMP
   instead of output/patched/DCOLYMP, using a card without an OMKIT folder.

Full guide and troubleshooting: docs/platform/om5m2/fw1100/install.md in
the OMKit repository, https://github.com/necrostaz/omkit
